Challenge: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots. The video and slides are alternative demo formats; the team should select one for the official demo field.

What the demo shows

  1. 0:00–0:03. Team KUETaguns introduces Agun Panjika: a fire history you can inspect.
  2. 0:03–0:07. Bring satellite observations into one inspectable history. A raw sensor join is compared with a harmonized Bangladesh record.
  3. 0:07–0:12. Spin Earth and tap a country. Recent NASA observations cover 242 countries and territories, with satellite feeds kept separate.
  4. 0:12–0:18. Reveal how corrected history compares with observed VIIRS in an annual past-only historical experiment.
  5. 0:18–0:24. Weekly error falls 35.10% in Chin and 21.45% in Punjab–Haryana against the previous release, across all 156 original weeks per area.
  6. 0:24–0:29. Save a country on a phone and share dated evidence to support learning, research and community monitoring.
