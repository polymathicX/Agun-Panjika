\subsection{What acceptance means}
Tests establish software behavior, not scientific validity. Chromium installation checks do not certify physical phones. A successful hosted global refresh does not verify the full heavy science pipeline. A public demo does not perform the team's official submission.

The updated workflow improves Chin from 136.414 to 88.530 weekly MAE (35.10\%) and Punjab--Haryana from 313.525 to 246.265 (21.45\%), retaining all 156 original weeks. A strict support guard addresses tree saturation; annual past-only fitting adapts the observation relationship.

\begin{keypoint}{Keep the comparison honest}
Chin's monthly baseline remains better. Bangladesh and Nepal worsen by about 2\%. No per-area method is chosen after inspecting these results. The chart reports those trade-offs.
\end{keypoint}

\subsection{Reference quality is a separate correction}
NASA documents a 2--6 November 2024 acquisition outage during a major Punjab error. Raw timestamps corroborate the gap. A fixed global long-outage rule flags 14 test weeks identically for every method. The app publishes both the 142-week comparable scores and every raw observation/all-week score. Quality filtering is not advertised as a prediction improvement.

\subsection{What remains unproven}
Each target year fits only labels through two years earlier and calibrates on the preceding year. Its 49--51 calibration weeks give limited interval evidence. The historical years were known during development and alternatives were explored. This is a walk-forward replay, not an untouched final holdout. Wider independent evaluation and practitioner testing remain necessary.

\ReportPage{Research work packages and exit criteria}{sec:research}
\subsection{1. Locked evaluation and uncertainty}
Freeze preprocessing, features, weights, area choices and primary metrics. Pre-register a new temporal/geographic evaluation; fit calibration only inside permitted partitions. Publish every baseline, regional/seasonal error, interval coverage and failure. A reviewer should confirm that final labels were not used to choose the method.

\subsection{2. Independent industrial-source and coverage labels}
Collect fresh blind positive/negative/uncertain labels independently of the filter, including likely vegetation fires. Measure retained-fire sensitivity and precision/recall with justified uncertainty. Historical sensor-span zero filling needs stronger retrieval/quality context before interpreting every zero as a clear observation.

\subsection{3. Geographic and scale transport}
Choose additional contrasting fire regimes independently of expected performance. Compare simple baselines and local fits. Validate at the intended spatial scale before providing arbitrary-polygon historical estimates. Worldwide recent observations can remain available while the historical evidence grows.

\subsection{4. A real comprehension and practitioner pilot}
Use the existing task protocol to test whether people identify source dates, observed versus estimated values, interval meaning and an appropriate next action. Record consent, anonymized outcomes, interpretation errors and revisions. Blank protocol rows are not results; no adoption or prevention claims should precede evidence.

\subsection{5. Prospective outlook evaluation}
Archive actual issue-time forecasts and outputs. Use rolling-origin fitting, independent calibration and honest baselines. Evaluate alert usefulness and delivery before operational warning claims. Observed-future-weather hindcasts cannot supply that evidence.

\subsection{6. Devices and access}
Test physical low-end Android/iPhone, intermittent connectivity and assistive technologies. Record memory/interaction behavior and task completion. Broader translations should be reviewed by speakers familiar with the scientific terms.

\begin{keypoint}{Work that cannot be completed by code alone}
Untouched labels, independent practitioners, actual study responses and future observations must be collected. They have not been fabricated to close a feature checklist.
\end{keypoint}

\ReportPage{Practical value, mentor decisions and submission}{sec:next}
\subsection{A useful repeat journey}
The watchlist and dated country brief give a person a reason to return. Students can investigate their own country; community groups can share a traceable source; researchers and land managers can inspect seasonality and sensor changes. These are intended uses, not measured field benefits.

Add push alerts or agency integrations only when a practitioner identifies a decision they improve and source quality supports it. Arbitrary-polygon reconstruction remains withheld until validated at that scale; the official challenge describes drawn areas as optional. The current country globe and four historical packages provide honest supported selection.

\subsection{Three decisions to ask our mentor for}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Which single research question and primary metric should guide the next locked evaluation?
\item Which remote-sensing reviewer or practitioner could help examine failures, filtering and interval interpretation?
\item Which bounded user task should the first real pilot assess, and what outcome would justify continuing?
\end{enumerate}
Publication potential should follow the missing evidence. A focused technical report may be useful once the evaluation, filtering and practical-use questions are answered.

\subsection{Submission package and team action}
The package includes a project-page draft, complete brief, model card, public inputs, source/license acknowledgments, visible AI-assistance statement and two compliant demo alternatives. The team must select one demo format, check all public links, confirm the six members, review declarations and click \textbf{Submit for Judging} in the official interface. Check its confirmation; a Git push is not a submission.

The official guide limits the demo to seven slides including title/intro or a video of at most 30 seconds. The new deck has seven pages and the video lasts 29 seconds. The older 2:42 local video is background only. Current rules and source links are recorded in the official-guidance note; confirm any earlier local cutoff with the Local Lead.
