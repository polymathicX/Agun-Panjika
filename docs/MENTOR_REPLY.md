# Short message to accompany the project package

Thank you, Sir. We have updated our repository with the project overview, scientific methods and results, current screenshots, limitations and proposed global-round work.

**Agun Panjika** makes MODIS and VIIRS fire records comparable over time, helping users understand whether a fire season is unusual. Our Bangladesh prototype includes bilingual calendars, maps, uncertainty and reports. Our internal evaluation reports about 25% lower weekly error than a fitted single-factor baseline; we want to strengthen independent validation before making broader claims.

For the next stage, we propose independently evaluated additional regions, clearer sensor-transition comparisons, and a small practitioner/user study.
