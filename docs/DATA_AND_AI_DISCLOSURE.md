# Data, derived outputs and development assistance

Team KUETaguns · 15 November 2026

**The project concept and competition responsibility belong to KUETaguns.** OpenAI Codex assisted this release's implementation, debugging, review, documentation, plotting and demo assembly. Instructions covered the team's requested global globe, PWA, reproducible evaluation and mentor/submission materials; inputs included repository code, public NASA observations and cited guides. This records the current release assistance, not an independently verified tool history for the earlier local prototype. The team should review the final materials before making the official submission.

The solution also uses machine learning: negative-binomial regression, LightGBM and conformal interval calibration. The model cards distinguish the weather-assisted Bangladesh model from the portable detector-only temporal experiments. Model estimates and intervals are derived analysis, not satellite measurements.

Raw observations come from the cited NASA FIRMS feeds and archives. No synthetic fire detections, fabricated user-study results, claimed agency adoption or invented impact measurements are used. Country assignment and counts are computed by the pipeline; generalized boundaries and unmatched points limit their interpretation. Source URLs, dates and hashes are in the data packages.

New product images are captures of the actual application. Earth/cloud texture attribution remains with Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0; these textures are static geographic context. Native data figures and diagrams are generated from the cited artifacts. The short demo uses actual app captures and captions, with an assistance notice. It has no synthetic narrator or impersonated person. No official NASA logo was generated or modified for this release.
